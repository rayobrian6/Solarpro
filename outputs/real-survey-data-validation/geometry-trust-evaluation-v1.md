# Geometry Trust Evaluation Report V1

Geometry Intelligence V1 introduces three primary trust scores and one discrepancy score. The geometry confidence score evaluates parser evidence quality, missing evidence, canonical geometry validity, projection origin health, and OSS comparison invalidity signals. The topology integrity score emphasizes invalid polygons, self-intersections, duplicate edges, near-zero projections, overlaps, clipping failures, and discrepancy penalties. The readiness trust score evaluates native CAD readiness, required review items, blocking issues, and accumulated risk impact. The discrepancy severity score summarizes native-vs-OSS disagreement impact without modifying readiness.

The scoring model is intentionally deterministic and lightweight. It is not an ML model, not a prediction system, and not an authority engine. It is a transparent weighted scoring utility designed to turn already-existing evidence into review-first operational insight. The weights are deliberately simple and explainable so future reviewers can reason about why a survey received a lower trust score.

The implemented risk taxonomy includes low confidence geometry, topology disagreement, unsupported polygon structures, overlapping geometry, malformed obstruction geometry, missing critical orientation data, invalid unit normalization, conflicting survey evidence, near-zero geometry projections, and readiness downgrade conditions. Current fixtures exercised six of those categories directly: overlapping geometry, low confidence geometry, near-zero geometry projections, readiness downgrade conditions, unsupported polygon structures, and conflicting survey evidence. Malformed obstruction geometry remains represented as an explicit taxonomy category for future obstruction polygon DTO support; the current obstruction model does not contain obstruction rings.

Fixture evaluation showed clean geometry receives full trust scores: geometry confidence 100, topology integrity 100, readiness trust 100, discrepancy severity 0, geometry risk low, and review urgency none. Self-intersecting or unsupported structures become critical and require blocker review. Overlap conditions become routine review without changing native readiness. Missing evidence and readiness review items degrade readiness trust without implying geometry mutation or automatic downgrade.

The most important trust improvement is that geometry health is no longer binary. Before this layer, a survey could be native `cad_ready` while still carrying adapter-only concerns such as overlap, near-zero projection, or bounding-box false-positive disagreement. Geometry Intelligence V1 makes those conditions visible through deterministic scoring and explainable risk signals while preserving SolarPro native authority.

This phase also improves readiness explainability. Operators can now see whether a readiness state is supported by high geometry confidence or whether it is carrying review-only trust degradation. The lightweight operator summary exposes geometry trust score, topology integrity score, readiness trust score, risk level, discrepancy severity, review urgency, integrity summary, top risk categories, and review actions. This is sufficient for operational triage without building a large UI or creating a new workflow surface.
